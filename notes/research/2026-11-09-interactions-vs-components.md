Asked by: user for none

## Answer

This is expected from the two analyses: `interactions.md` is generated by the browser crawler and records only screens and controls it actually reached, while `components.md` also inspects the HTML source and JavaScript-rendered functions. Therefore `components.md` lists Home, Trip Setup, Telemetry, Analytics, and Scores even though `interactions.md` contains only the initial `#app` screen. The source confirms these states are rendered by `renderHome`, `renderTelemetry`, `renderAnalytics`, and `renderScores`, and `ui-states.md` explicitly marks them as source-JS states that the crawler did not reach.

- analysis/interactions.md § Screens — generated crawler output contains only `#app`; it says not to edit by hand
- analysis/components.md § HomePage through DriverScoresPage — source-JS screens and components are catalogued

## Remaining uncertainty

The documents do not decide whether the crawler output should be extended to authenticated flows and produce separate screenshots; the client/product owner must approve that measurement scope.
